# Referring Colleagues to ReadyPackets

When the ReadyPackets referral programme is active on your portal, you can invite colleagues using your personal referral link and earn account credit after they complete their first paid order.

## Finding and Sharing Your Referral Link
1. Sign in to your ReadyPackets customer account and open **Account -> Referrals**.
2. Under **Share your referral link**, review the current programme reward and copy either:
   - **Your referral code**: A 10-character code without look-alike letters or digits (such as `0`/`O` or `1`/`I`).
   - **Your referral link**: Opens the ReadyPackets registration page with your code pre-attached.
3. To send an invitation directly from the portal, select **E-mail my link**, enter your colleague's work e-mail address, and select **Send invitation**. ReadyPackets sends a standard transactional invitation containing your referral link. To protect recipients from unwanted mail, invitations cannot include custom message text and are subject to daily and per-recipient sending limits.

## How Referral Credits Work
- **30-day attribution window**: When someone opens your referral link, ReadyPackets holds your referral code in a first-party cookie on that browser for up to 30 days until they finish creating their account.
- **Qualification**: A referral qualifies once your colleague's new ReadyPackets account completes its **first paid invoice in full**. Self-referrals (including aliases or accounts under the same customer reference) are not eligible.
- **Review and account credit**: Depending on portal policy, qualified referrals are either approved automatically or reviewed by a ReadyPackets administrator. Once approved and applied, the credit appears under **Summary** and **Referral history** on **Account -> Referrals**.
- **Privacy**: Your referral history shows status dates, reward amounts, and a masked account hint (for example, `Account ending 42`) without exposing another customer's e-mail address or order details.
