# What your order needs before the next step

Customer help article (draft, increment 04). Owner: orders_engineer.

Some orders move through **phases**, such as Intake, Review and Delivery. The progress rail at the top of your order shows the phase you are in and the phases that are done.

## Things we may ask for

Under **Needed before the next step**, your order page lists what is still missing for the current step. Each item is marked done, to do, blocked or optional.

- **Uploads.** These are files we need from you, for example a signed contract or an audio recording. Select **Upload** to go to the Files tab. The item tells you how many files are needed and which file types are accepted.
- **Acknowledgements.** These are short texts you read and accept. Select **Read and accept** to open the full text. Read it, tick "I have read and accept this text", and type your full name if we ask for it. Then select **Accept**. We record the date and time and the exact text you saw. An acceptance cannot be changed later. If you think something is wrong, contact us.

Items marked "Our team will confirm this" are for us to complete. You do not need to do anything.

## Why a phase is locked

When your order moves on to a new phase, the earlier phase is locked. The files and acceptances in it stay the way we reviewed them. You can still view and download everything. If something needs to change, contact us. Our team can unlock the phase for you and will tell you when it is open again.

## Orders shared with you

Our team may give another person access to one of your orders, or to all of them. Both of you are told when this happens. A **viewer** can see and download. A **contributor** can also upload and accept on your behalf.

## Accessibility

These pages are designed and tested against WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark mode. The full text of an acknowledgement is in a scrollable area that you can reach with the keyboard. If anything gets in your way, contact us and we will help.
