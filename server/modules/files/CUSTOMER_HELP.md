# Upload files or record audio for an order

Open the order's Files tab. If a requirement is waiting for a file, choose it in Upload to. A general upload does not satisfy a workflow requirement. The form shows the allowed formats and limits; only people authorized to view the order can access files in a workflow slot. If access has changed or the order is locked, contact your ReadyPackets team.

When recording is enabled and your browser supports an approved format, choose Start recording and respond to the browser's microphone prompt. You can pause, resume, stop, and listen before choosing Use this recording. The clip stays on your device until you press Upload file. You can also select an existing audio file if you do not want to use the microphone. Include a plain-text transcript when you can, so readers who cannot listen can still use the information.

After upload, your original remains available. A WebM copy may appear when conversion completes; a failed conversion does not remove the original. Use Listen to load a recording and its supplied transcript, or download the file if your browser cannot play it.
