# Cookies and your choices (draft help article)

ReadyPackets uses a small number of cookies and similar tools. Some are needed for the site to work. Others
are optional, and they only run if you allow them.

## What we use

- **Necessary** — keeps you signed in, protects forms against forgery and remembers your cookie choice.
  These are always on.
- **Preferences** — remembers settings such as layout choices.
- **Analytics** — PostHog helps us understand which pages are used, so we can improve them.
- **Session replay** — Microsoft Clarity records anonymised sessions (no typed passwords or form values) to
  help us find problems.

The full list, with each cookie's name, provider, purpose and duration, is in **Manage cookie preferences**.

## Making or changing a choice

- When you first visit, choose **Reject all**, **Accept all** or **Manage choices** in the banner at the top
  of the page.
- You can change your mind at any time: use **Manage cookie preferences** in the footer, or go to
  **Account → Privacy and cookies** when signed in.
- If your browser sends **Global Privacy Control** or **Do Not Track**, we treat that as "reject all". You can
  still allow tools yourself.
- We ask again when our cookie policy changes, and at the latest after about a year.

## When you withdraw consent

We stop the tool straight away and remove the cookies this site can reach. Cookies that a provider stored on
its own website can only be removed from your browser's settings.

## Your record

Signed-in customers can see the history of their choices and download it from **Account → Privacy and
cookies**. We keep a record of each choice as proof that we respected it. It contains no IP address. If your
account is deleted, your name is removed from these records.

Questions? Contact us through **Support**.
