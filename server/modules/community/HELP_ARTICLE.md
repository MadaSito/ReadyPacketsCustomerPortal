# Using the community forum (draft)

The community is a place to ask questions about ReadyPackets and share tips with other customers. Our support team takes part too; their posts carry a **Staff** label.

If you do not see **Community** in the menu, your provider has not switched it on.

## Getting started

1. Choose **Community** in the menu under Support.
2. The first time you post, pick a display name. This is what other members see. Do not use your e-mail address, phone number or account number.
3. Choose a category, then **Start a topic**, or open a topic and reply.

## Writing a post

- Plain text only. Blank lines start a new paragraph. Web addresses that begin with `https://` become links.
- Do not share personal information, order details, passwords or payment details. For anything about your own account, contact support instead.
- You can edit your post for a short time after publishing it.

## Why is my post waiting for review?

To keep the forum free of spam, posts from new accounts, posts with many links and posts containing certain words are checked by a moderator before others can see them. You will see "Waiting for review" on your post until then. There are also limits on how many posts you can make in an hour or a day.

## Helpful posts, following and reporting

- Select **Helpful** on a reply that answered your question.
- **Follow** a topic to be notified when someone replies. The notification contains a link, not the reply text.
- Select **Report** if a post is spam, abusive, off topic or shares personal information. Moderators review every report. A post that several members report is hidden until a moderator looks at it.

## Accessibility

The community pages are designed to work with a keyboard and screen readers, in light and dark themes, and on small screens. We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA and check the pages with automated accessibility tests. If something is hard to use, please tell support so we can fix it.
