# Your security settings (customer help draft)

Open **Settings → Account → Security**.

## Password
Change your password by entering your current password and a new one of at least 15 characters. Long phrases are easiest to remember.

## Two-step verification
Manage your authenticator app, passkeys and recovery codes.

## Where you're signed in
See each device signed in to your account, with its approximate location and last activity. **Sign out** ends one device; **Sign out everywhere else** ends all other devices. If you do not recognise a device, sign it out and change your password.

## Recent sign-in activity
Your last 20 sign-ins, sign-outs and failed attempts. Several failed attempts you did not make may mean someone is guessing your password — change it and contact support.

## Why was I signed out?
Your organisation may limit how many devices can stay signed in, how long sessions last, or where you can sign in from. During planned maintenance, sign-in is temporarily paused and a message explains when it will be back.
