# Using Your Cart and Checking Out (Help Draft)

Customer help article draft located in [`server/modules/cart/CUSTOMER_HELP.md`](../../server/modules/cart/CUSTOMER_HELP.md). Covers adding packet tiers and add-ons, automatic bundle pricing, checkout billing and policy acceptance, read-only payment confirmation polling on `/checkout/return`, and explicit `POST` cart restoration on `/checkout/cancel`. All screens are designed and tested against WCAG 2.2 AA.
