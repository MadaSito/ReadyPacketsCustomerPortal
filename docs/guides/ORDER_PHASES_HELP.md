# Working through your order's phases

For customers. Many orders are organised into named **phases**. The order page guides you through them one at a time.

## Progress bar and phase list

- **Progress bar.** It sits at the top of the order and shows how far along your order is, as a percentage. Screen readers hear it in words, for example "40 percent, step 2 of 5: Intake". The bar moves smoothly, except when your device asks for reduced motion.
- **How progress is counted.** Some phases count more than others. The bar stays below 100 percent until the order is finished. Sometimes our team sets the progress directly; when they do, the bar shows the higher of the two values.
- **Phase list.** **Order phases** lists every phase with its state: Not started, In progress, Submitted, In review, Accepted, Reopened or Not open yet. The state is always written out, never shown by colour alone.
- **Earlier and later phases.** You can open earlier phases to read what you sent, but you cannot change them. Later phases show "Opens when the previous phase is complete." Use **Back to the current phase** to return.
- **Canceled orders.** The bar keeps its last value and says the order was canceled. The phases can still be read.

## What a phase may ask of you

Each phase shows its **Instructions** and only the actions it needs:

- **Documents.** The upload form shows how many files you may still add and the size limit.
- **Record audio** or **Upload audio.** The allowance includes the total recording time, which the server measures after you upload. The portal keeps your original audio and adds a WebM/Opus copy for playback. The copy is a re-encode, not an identical file.
- **Questions from our team.** Answer each question. Questions marked **Required** must be answered before you can continue. Every time you save, a new version is kept; earlier answers are never overwritten. Never put passwords or sign-in codes in an answer.
- **Files from our team.** These appear when our team has published them for you.

If a phase has nothing for you to do, it says so. For example, "This step completes when your payment is confirmed," or "Our team is working on this step. Nothing is needed from you right now."

## Next, or Submit and lock

- **Next** finishes the phase and moves you on. You can still read it later.
- **Submit and lock** finishes the phase and locks it. The page warns you first: "After you submit, this phase is locked for changes."
- **Acknowledgement.** Some phases ask you to confirm a statement before you submit. Tick **I have read and accept this**. If the text changed while you were reading, the page asks you to read it again.
- **Before you can continue,** the page tells you if something is missing: a required answer, a file, or an agreement or policy shown at the top of the page.

## After you submit

- Our team reviews the phase. You may see **Our team accepted this phase**, or **Our team asked for changes in this phase**.
- If changes are needed, an administrator reopens the phase so you can make them. The page then says "This phase was reopened. Make your changes, then continue."
- A locked phase says "This phase is locked. An administrator can reopen it if something needs to change."

## Downloading a phase's files

Use **Download phase files (ZIP)** (the button may name the phase) to get every current file in a phase you can see as one ZIP file.

- **What is in it.** Only the current versions are included, and audio is the WebM/Opus copy.
- **Waiting for it.** The page tells you while the ZIP is being prepared.
- **Limits.** A ZIP holds at most 250 files and 500 MiB, and it stays available for 30 minutes.
- **If files change.** If something changes before you download, prepare the ZIP again.

## Accessibility

The wizard works with a keyboard and on screens as narrow as 320 px, in light and dark themes. It is designed and tested against WCAG 2.2 AA. No screen-reader pass has been done for this release yet.
