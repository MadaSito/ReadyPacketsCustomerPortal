# Customer help: maintenance

During maintenance, you may see a notice on the sign-in or registration page. New customer sign-ins, registrations, and single sign-on may be temporarily unavailable. If the notice lists an expected end, that time is in UTC. Try again after maintenance ends.

If you are already signed in, you may be able to stay signed in. If your session ends during maintenance, sign in again once the notice clears. You do not need to reset your password to resolve a maintenance notice.
