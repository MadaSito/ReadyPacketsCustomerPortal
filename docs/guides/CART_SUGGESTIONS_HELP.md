# Help: Suggestions in your cart

<!-- Adapted by platform_engineer from server/modules/cart/CUSTOMER_HELP.md (recovery_ops_engineer).
Suggestions appear only when the portal administrator has switched them on. -->

Your **Cart** (`/cart`) may show a **Recommended for your cart** section with up to three optional suggestions:

- an add-on that is not yet in your cart;
- a higher tier of a packet that is already in your cart, showing the exact price difference (for example "+$40.00");
- one or two more packets that would unlock the bundle discount, showing the saving.

Nothing is added without your action. Choose **Add to cart** or **Upgrade** to accept a suggestion, or **Dismiss** to hide it for this cart. Prices are always worked out by ReadyPackets from the current catalogue. If an item is withdrawn, the suggestion disappears.

To use a discount code as well, see [Using a discount code](./DISCOUNT_CODES_HELP.md). Your cart applies either the bundle discount or the code, whichever saves you more, unless the code says it can be combined.
