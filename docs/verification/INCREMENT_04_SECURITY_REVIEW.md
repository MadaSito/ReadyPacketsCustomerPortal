- **J**: The cutover is verified with component mocks, not vendor traffic in Chromium. Check the real browser for off/default with no optional storage or network requests; equal reject prominence; GPC/DNT; separate categories; withdrawal/expiry/version change; reload after taint; and exact anonymous `/privacy` and `/terms` only, without query or fragment. Withdrawal applies locally before teardown or ledger submission; verify ledger delivery during reload and fallbacks when local storage writes are unavailable. Confirm CSP, modal interaction and user/anonymous ledger scopes.
