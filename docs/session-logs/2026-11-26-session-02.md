### Decisions

These are recorded in [INCREMENT_08_CONTRACT.md](../engineering/INCREMENT_08_CONTRACT.md), D1–D8.
- **Payment booking.** Decision 12 of increment 07 ("never mark paid without a signed webhook") is replaced. A payment is booked from either a verified signed webhook or an authoritative Stripe lookup made by this server with its own credentials. Both go through one idempotent booking function (D5).
