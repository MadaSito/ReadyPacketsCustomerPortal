### Lead QA report and rulings (2026-11-25)

The lead's rulings, now reflected in the ADRs and guides:

- **Progress:**
  - every phase counts by its weight (1–100, default 1);
  - derived = floor(done weight × 100 ÷ total weight), capped at 99 until a `done` status (100);
  - shown = max(derived, explicit), and it goes down only on an audited administrator reopen;
  - `orders.increment07.startup` runs once at every boot and every 10 minutes.
- **Workflow:**
  - the first standard phase is labelled "Payment";
  - the audit event `order.stage.passed_by_status`;
  - `sessions.principalForJob` is the only way background jobs get a principal.
- **SAML:**
  - an upgraded configuration starts in `portal` MFA mode, and `claim` is recommended for new setups;
  - `entra` needs the typed words `ENTRA MANAGES MFA`;
  - switching protocol disables SSO until it is enabled again;
  - there is no IdP-initiated sign-on, single logout or encrypted assertions.
- **Stripe:**
  - activation needs the egress preset, an API check and a self-test for the current key and mode, then a confirmation, and live mode also needs `ACTIVATE LIVE PAYMENTS`;
  - an unmatched paid session with portal metadata raises `UNMATCHED_PORTAL_PAYMENT`;
  - a non-JSON body (400) or one over 256 KB (413) stops at the HTTP layer and is not logged as a rejection.
- **Files:**
  - format-3 customer uploads must name the current phase (`STAGE_REQUIRED`), and format 1/2 orders are unchanged;
  - a WAV upload keeps the original and adds a WebM/Opus copy;
  - the ZIP is a native form POST with a narrow `Origin: null` rule (`5a0a492`), and asking again resumes or reuses it;
  - ZIP files are named after the order reference (`7c6bb71`) and hold the transcoded copy.
- **Auto-queue:**
  - P101 is queued on a real payment record and never retroactively;
  - P201 is queued on the intake phase being submitted, after a delivered P101;
  - both are off by default per workflow;
  - pipeline signing is off by default, and new subscriptions are signed and start paused.
- **Integrations hub:** the cards show live state (`c720201`).
