## Prompt 28

Verbatim, with the owner's spelling kept. The owner attached three screenshots from their server, taken while signed in as the default administrator inside the customer portal. They are not stored in the repository.

> This is a message i got when i tried to create na order as an administrator. Also When adding the chart and checking out there is no option to apply a discount code. There is no option to switch from the customer portal to the admin portal for users who are staff or admins

### Causes found

- **Permission message.** The administrator was using the customer portal. The server only lets customer accounts own orders and carts, a zero-trust separation that stays. So step 3 of `/orders/new` and `/cart` answered "You do not have permission to perform this action".
- **No discount code field.** The field is shown only when discount codes are switched on at `/admin/promotions` (off when shipped) and the cart has an item. The administrator never reached a cart, because the cart was refused for their role.
- **No portal switch.** The way to switch portals existed only inside the avatar menu.

### Changes (client side; no server permission, schema or migration change)

- **Ordering for staff.** For staff and administrators, `/orders/new`, `/cart`, `/cart/checkout`, `/checkout/return` and `/checkout/cancel` now show a handoff panel, "Orders are placed for a customer". It offers **New order for a customer**, which goes to `/admin/orders?new=1` and opens the console dialog. That dialog accepts a discount code. No cart requests are made for staff. The customer navigation hides **Create an order** and **Cart** for staff.
- **Portal switch.** It is visible in the console top bar (medium screens and up) and the drawer as **Customer portal**. In the customer shell it is **Admin portal** or **Staff portal**, in the top bar, the drawer and a staff notice that cannot be dismissed.
  - Staff and administrators opening `/` land on `/admin`, unless this browser tab chose the customer portal. That choice is per tab and is cleared at sign-out.
  - Customers never see the switch.
- **Discount code hints.** Two hints were added:
  - empty cart: "Have a discount code? Add an item first to apply it here or at checkout.";
  - the order form's Review step: "Have a discount code? You can enter it in your cart and at checkout."
- **Scrollbars.** Both navigation rails now have a thin themed scrollbar, with a forced-colours fallback.

Commits: `cf9dfca`, `9266d15`, `c48ad2d`, `9d34253`.

## Prompt 29

> also this site should be 100% responsive for all devices (Laptops, Desktops, Mobile devices) and 100% ada compliant

### Result

- **Sweep.** The lead ran a responsive and accessibility sweep:
  - widths from 240 to 2560 px, in light and dark themes;
  - axe checks against WCAG 2.2 AA;
  - a manual keyboard and zoom pass.
- **Harness.** The QA tools gained an opt-in context-reuse mode and deferred retries (`8cae7f0`, see `scripts/qa/README.md`).
- **Fixes.** The sweep's findings were fixed in the shell, layout, accessibility and server commits listed below.
- **What is claimed.** The portal is designed and tested against WCAG 2.2 AA. This is not a claim of full responsiveness on every device or of ADA conformance. The limits are below.

### Sweep results

All runs used Chromium with synthetic QA data, against QA builds of `fix/increment-07-1`. The QA sweep runner reported the numbers, and the lead spot-checked them in a browser. During the sweep, the QA rate budgets were raised temporarily and then restored. That applied to the QA environment only and is not a product change.

**Full responsive matrix (first fix build).**
- 3,712 cases were scheduled: 232 route and role states × 8 widths (240, 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1440, 1920, 2560 px) × light and dark.
- 3,656 passed, 24 failed, and 32 were not checked at that point.
- The 24 failures were 12 distinct route states, and all but two failed only at 240 px. Two were within the WCAG reflow scope:
  - **Graph & SharePoint** overflowed by 8 px at 320 px;
  - the cart suggestion "Add-on" badge clipped at 1024 px.

**axe (WCAG 2.2 A and AA plus best practice), at 1280, 640 and 320 px in both themes.**

- The 33 cases with violations were:

  - 1 intermittent `target-size`, on the workflow version canvas.
- 712 cases had axe "incomplete" items that need human judgement: 704 colour contrast, 18 heading order and 6 `aria-hidden-focus`, and one case can have more than one. These items were not reviewed one by one.

**Fixes made after the first build.**
